Understand the requirement

What is GST InvoiceNow?

InvoiceNow is Singapore's nationwide e-invoicing network, built on the international Peppol standard. Under the GST InvoiceNow Requirement, GST-registered businesses send their invoice data directly to IRAS through an InvoiceNow-Ready Solution, instead of only sending PDFs to customers.

  • Faster GST audits and quicker GST refunds
  • Built-in checks that flag GST wrongly charged by non-GST-registered suppliers
  • Easier record-keeping, billing and payment processing
  • Structured e-invoices that reduce manual data entry and errors

When Does GST InvoiceNow Apply to You?

The requirement is rolled out in phases. The grant must be used before your own mandate deadline.

  1. 1 Apr 2026 Now active

    All new voluntary GST registrants

  2. 1 Apr 2028

    New compulsory GST registrants + existing businesses with annual supplies ≤ S$200,000

  3. 1 Apr 2029

    Existing businesses with annual supplies ≤ S$1,000,000

  4. 1 Apr 2030

    Existing businesses with annual supplies ≤ S$4,000,000

  5. 1 Apr 2031

    Existing businesses with annual supplies > S$4,000,000

Total annual supplies = standard-rated, zero-rated and exempt supplies (Box 4 of your GST return) made in all prescribed accounting periods ending in calendar year 2025. Not sure of your date? Download the implementation date calculator (Excel).

Eligibility

Does Your Business Qualify?

You can claim the S$1,000 GST InvoiceNow Transition Grant if you meet all of these:

  • Your business is GST-registered in Singapore.
  • Your total annual supplies in calendar year 2025 are S$4 million or less.
  • You have not used any InvoiceNow-Ready Solution (IRSP) or InvoiceNow connection in the 3 months before onboarding.
  • You subscribe to an IMDA-accredited IRSP solution, such as Netiquette, with payment for at least 12 months, after 26 February 2026.
  • You submit invoice data to IRAS after activating GST InvoiceNow, before your mandate deadline.
Above S$4 million in annual supplies? You may qualify for the S$5,000 Transition Grant or the S$25,000 Queen Bee Grant instead. Ask our team.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who gives out the S$1,000 grant?

The GST InvoiceNow Transition Grant is provided by IMDA together with IRAS. Netiquette, as an IMDA-accredited InvoiceNow-Ready Solution Provider, supports you through the requirements, but the grant is approved and paid by IMDA.

How do I know my total annual supplies?

Add up Box 4 of your GST returns (standard-rated, zero-rated and exempt supplies) for all prescribed accounting periods ending in calendar year 2025. If the total is S$4 million or less, you fall under the S$1,000 grant.

Can I take the FOC package and the S$1,000 grant at the same time?

No. They are separate schemes and you can only take one for the same subscription. Since the FOC package is already free, there is no cost for the grant to offset.

I'm on the FOC package. Can I claim the grant later?

Yes. After your FOC subscription ends, wait 3 months, then subscribe to a paid plan. You can claim the grant if you still meet all criteria and the grant window is still open.

Why do I need the Inventory module for InvoiceNow?

Netiquette's GST InvoiceNow feature is built inside the Inventory Management System (IMS). That's why the 3-in-1 Suite includes IMS together with Accounting and Payroll.

How long does onboarding take?

About 5 working days on average, from sign-up to your first GST InvoiceNow e-invoice. Simpler setups are faster.

Do I need technical knowledge to get a Peppol ID?

No. We handle and guide your Peppol ID registration with DataPost, our accredited access point partner, as part of onboarding.

My business is not GST-registered. Does this apply to me?

No. GST InvoiceNow and this grant are only for GST-registered businesses. If you are applying for GST registration now, reach out and we can help you prepare.

Where can I read the official grant terms?

See IMDA's InvoiceNow Grants page and the official grant FAQs.
